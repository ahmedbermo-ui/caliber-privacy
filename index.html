Caliber – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Caliber does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Each time you finish a test, the app saves the date, your estimated score range, the number of correct answers in each area, and how long the test took. This list of results is kept only on your device so the Home screen can show your recent results.
  • A test you leave before the last question is not saved.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account, no sign-in, and does not ask for your name or email address.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking code.
  • It makes no network requests and asks for no permissions.
  • It does not know who you are.

About the result

Caliber gives an estimate for interest, not a clinical assessment. Nothing about your result leaves your phone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a feature that needs to send data is ever added), this page will be updated before that feature is released.

Contact

Use the developer contact details shown on the Caliber page in Google Play.
