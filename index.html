Caliber – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy covers the app published on Google Play as Caliber (inside the app it may show its new name, Rulesight).

The app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. Everything you do in it stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your tests and challenges: the puzzles you were given, the answers you chose, how long each one took, and your score range.
  • Your puzzle rating and its history, your mistake notebook, your daily puzzle results and how many practice puzzles you did each day.
  • Your settings: theme, language, puzzle style and timed mode.
  • Whether you have bought the full version, so it keeps working offline.

None of this is sent anywhere by the app. If backup is turned on for your phone, your phone may include this data in your own Google or Apple backup, as it does for other apps.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase made through Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone). The store handles the payment; the app never sees your card or payment details. To know whether you own the full version, and to restore it, the app asks the store directly. That is the only reason the app can use the internet, and the only thing it uses the internet for.

Because of this, on Android the app has these permissions: Internet and network state (to reach Google Play) and Google Play billing. It asks for no other permissions.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account, no sign-in, and does not ask for your name or email address.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking code.
  • It does not know who you are.

Sharing

If you tap Share (a result card, a daily puzzle result or a challenge code), the app opens your phone's share menu and you choose where it goes. A challenge code only describes a set of puzzles; it contains nothing about you.

About the score

The app gives an unofficial IQ-style score from original puzzles, for practice and interest. It is not a clinical or official IQ assessment. Nothing about your score leaves your device unless you share it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a feature that needs to send data is ever added), this page will be updated before that feature is released.

Contact

Use the developer contact details shown on the Caliber page in Google Play.
